# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first version.

- Decks of slides whose numbers are never stored. A slide's number is worked
  out from its place in the deck every time anything is drawn, so the sorter,
  the number on the slide, the agenda, a "see slide 7" in the middle of another
  slide, the presenter view, the PDF and "go to slide" are all right the
  instant a slide moves - after a drag, a cut and paste, a delete, a duplicate,
  a change of section, hiding a slide, or an undo of any of it. A
  cross-reference remembers which slide it points at rather than what number
  that slide had; deleting the slide it names is reported by Check.
- Hidden slides are skipped in the numbering, or kept in it, as the deck says.
- An editor: a slide sorter that drags to reorder, multi-selects and folds
  sections away; a canvas with smart guides, snapping, equal-spacing hints and
  <kbd>Alt</kbd> to place freely; an inspector; a command palette on
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>; unlimited undo; and autosave.
- Layouts a deck's slides share, the way a document's paragraphs share styles.
  Changing a layout changes every slide using it, unless that slide has changed
  the same thing itself, and "Back to layout" forgets the difference.
- Text, pictures, icons, charts, tables, code, big numbers, shapes, lines and
  arrows, generated graphics, and the fields that fill themselves in: the slide
  number, the date, the footer, a reference to another slide, and an agenda
  built from the deck's sections.
- Deck styles generated by rules from a seed, with no AI in them - a
  twelve-column grid, one scale of type, text measured for its words, and
  colours chosen by checked contrast. Generate another, the <kbd>G</kbd> key,
  Previous, and a palette you can hold while the layouts change. Six layouts at
  a time for one slide, and Six more. A whole new style for a deck that already
  has slides tells you first how much moves and how much has nowhere to go.
- Present mode: full screen, transitions that are only none, fade or slide
  across, a laser pointer, <kbd>B</kbd> and <kbd>W</kbd> to blank the screen,
  and a number and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go to a slide. A presenter view in a
  second window with the next slide, the notes, an elapsed timer and the clock.
- Check: everything that will not look right, worst first, and clicking an item
  goes to it.
- PDFs written from exactly what the screen draws, with no dependencies: one
  slide to a page at the size the slides are, plus notes and handouts two,
  three or six to a page. PNG per slide at screen, Full HD or 4K.
- Push, pull and history as in Newsx: bundles in a shared folder, a three-way
  merge that asks only about what both people changed, and a pull preview that
  draws the slides it changes.
- Seven palettes by role, four pairings of the three PDF-standard faces, a line
  icon set of more than a hundred drawings, fourteen kinds of chart and ten
  kinds of generated graphic - all carried over from Newsx, with a header in
  each file naming where it came from.
- A window of its own through pywebview, or the browser.
- A test that fails if anybody puts an AI in it.
